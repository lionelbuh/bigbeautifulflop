Extortion at the highest level

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 7 Nov 2026 · 12:45 PM ET

Original

The Democrats are going around threatening a lot of people, including the Attorney General of the United States, and others, with Impeachment and many other forms of punishment, even though they admit they have no reason to do so, and there have been no impeachable acts committed, or even thought of, by the victims of this illegal process. This is extortion at the highest level, and they should be held accountable for this. Also, they’re threatening our Donors, some of the most respected people anywhere in the World, with imprisonment, and many other levels of punishment, just because they happen to be Republicans, or Republican supporters. This is Weaponization and Lawfare at another level. The Senator from Michigan, Elissa Slotkin, is saying that she is going to go after all young people that believe in Republican Values, and that give even the slightest inclination to be a Republican. The imprisonment should be against these EXTORTIONISTS, and Republicans should get smart, fast! Threatening letters have been sent to many people, all innocent, from Democrat Political Leaders. By copy of this statement, I am ordering the Department of Justice to look into this criminal act, immediately! President DONALD J. TRUMP

The American PresidentThe presidential version: how a statesmanlike president would say it

Satire

Several members of my administration, including the Attorney General, have been threatened with impeachment, and some Republican supporters say they have received letters warning them of legal consequences. I believe these threats are unjustified, and I am concerned that political disagreements are being turned into legal threats.

Impeachment is a serious constitutional process, reserved for serious misconduct, and it should never be used as a means of pressure. Anyone who believes they are being threatened unlawfully should contact law enforcement. It will be for the Department of Justice, acting independently, to determine whether any law was broken. I ask the leaders of both parties to lower the temperature.

Version française

Plusieurs membres de mon administration, dont le procureur général des États-Unis, ont été menacés de destitution, et des sympathisants républicains disent avoir reçu des lettres les avertissant de conséquences judiciaires. Je considère ces menaces comme injustifiées, et je m’inquiète de voir des désaccords politiques se transformer en menaces judiciaires.

La destitution est une procédure constitutionnelle grave, réservée à des fautes graves ; elle ne doit jamais servir de moyen de pression. Quiconque s’estime menacé illégalement doit s’adresser aux forces de l’ordre. Il appartiendra au ministère de la Justice, agissant en toute indépendance, de déterminer si la loi a été enfreinte. J’appelle les dirigeants des deux partis à apaiser le ton.

Who’s right? We’ll see. But shouting “to jail” at every turn doesn’t really work, does it?
