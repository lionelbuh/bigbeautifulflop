Send them directly to me

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 7 Nov 2026 · 2:35 PM ET

Original

The threats being made by the Democrat Party against innocent Republican Donors and Party Members are absolutely unprecedented, and totally illegal. Please report them to the Department of Justice, IMMEDIATELY! If you would like, send them directly to me, your President. These SLEAZEBAGS should go to jail for what they are doing. The Senate must act, and stop being pushed around to the point of the Republican Party losing Elections, and worse! Likewise, the DOJ and House of Representatives. Thank you for your attention to this matter. President DONALD J. TRUMP

Source: Truth Social, @realDonaldTrump

The American PresidentThe presidential version: how a statesmanlike president would say it

Satire

I am concerned by reports that Republican donors and party members have received threatening messages. Intimidation has no place in our democracy, whoever it targets.

Anyone who receives a threat should report it to local law enforcement or to the FBI. The Department of Justice will assess each case independently, on the facts and the law. And I ask Congress to make sure that citizens of every party can support the candidates of their choice without fear.

Version française

Je suis préoccupé par les informations selon lesquelles des donateurs et des membres du Parti républicain auraient reçu des messages menaçants. L’intimidation n’a pas sa place dans notre démocratie, quelle qu’en soit la cible.

Toute personne qui reçoit une menace doit la signaler à la police ou au FBI. Le ministère de la Justice examinera chaque cas en toute indépendance, au regard des faits et du droit. Et je demande au Congrès de veiller à ce que les citoyens de tous les partis puissent soutenir les candidats de leur choix sans crainte.
