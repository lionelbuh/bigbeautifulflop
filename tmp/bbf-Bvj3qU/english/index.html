The very stable genius

★

The Presidential VersionHow a real president would say it

Satire

I have seen the questions raised this week about my fitness for office, and I understand why Americans want to hear from their President directly. Questions like these deserve an answer, not an insult.

I have been fortunate in my life. I built a business, I found success in television, and the American people entrusted me with this office in my first campaign. I don’t take that as proof of my own brilliance. I take it as a responsibility.

The best answer I can give to doubts about my judgement is the work itself, and I invite every American to judge me by it.

Version française

J’ai pris connaissance des questions soulevées cette semaine sur ma capacité à exercer mes fonctions, et je comprends que les Américains veuillent entendre leur président s’exprimer directement. De telles questions méritent une réponse, pas une insulte.

La vie m’a beaucoup donné : j’ai bâti une entreprise, j’ai connu le succès à la télévision, et les Américains m’ont confié cette fonction dès ma première candidature. Je n’y vois pas la preuve de mon génie, mais une responsabilité.

La meilleure réponse que je puisse apporter aux doutes sur mon jugement, c’est le travail accompli. J’invite chaque Américain à me juger sur pièces.

VaudoisVaud: Morges, Lausanne, Yverdon

Satire

Well. Some people are saying I’ve lost my marbles. I’m not saying anything, mind you. I’d be better off keeping quiet. But still.

I had a business that did all right, I did a bit of TV, and then I became president on the first go. So, genius… we’ll see. I wouldn’t say genius. I’d say it’s fine. It works.

And stable, too: three glasses of the local white on a vineyard terrace and I’m still standing. Come on, no rush, the lake isn’t on fire. We’ll talk about it later, yeah?

Covfefe

★

The Presidential VersionHow a real president would say it

Satire

Despite persistent criticism in the press, my administration remains focused on the work the American people elected us to do. A free press is entitled to its criticism, and we are entitled to answer it with results.

Correction: an earlier message from this account was sent incomplete. We regret the error.

Version française

Malgré les critiques persistantes de la presse, mon administration reste concentrée sur la mission que les Américains lui ont confiée. Une presse libre a le droit de critiquer ; nous avons le devoir de répondre par des résultats.

Rectificatif : un précédent message de ce compte a été publié incomplet. Nous prions nos lecteurs de nous en excuser.

Hey folks! Despite everything the papers say about me all day long… covfefe.

Besides, across the river, in the German-speaking part, they got it right away. They said “Exactly.” Come on, let’s finish the fondue before it gets cold.

Nobody knew health care could be so complicated

★

The Presidential VersionHow a real president would say it

Satire

Health care is one of the most complex systems in American life. It touches every family, every employer and every hospital in the country, and that is exactly why getting reform right matters more than getting it done fast.

Doctors, patients and many of the governors in this room have understood that complexity for years. We intend to learn from them, and we still intend to deliver a better law.

Version française

Le système de santé est l’un des plus complexes de la vie américaine. Il concerne chaque famille, chaque employeur et chaque hôpital du pays ; c’est précisément pourquoi il importe davantage de bien le réformer que de le réformer vite.

Les médecins, les patients et bon nombre des gouverneurs ici présents connaissent cette complexité depuis longtemps. Nous entendons apprendre d’eux, et nous restons déterminés à proposer une meilleure loi.

NeuchâteloisNeuchâtel, La Chaux-de-Fonds

Satire

Nobody knew health care was so complicated? In this canton we’ve known for ages: we’ve spent years arguing about whether the hospital should be up in the mountains or down by the lake.

We make watches with three hundred parts that fit inside a thimble. So complicated things, we know about.

But a health system is more complicated than a watch with complications. And on top of that, it doesn’t even tell the time.
