The Swiss President

The Swiss PresidentA President of the Swiss Confederation

Satire

The Federal Council does not comment on the internal politics of friendly countries.

We would only recall that in Switzerland there is no impeachment of a member of the government: Parliament simply decides, every four years, whether to re-elect them. And the people vote four times a year, which leaves very little time for threatening letters.

Version française

Le Conseil fédéral ne commente pas la politique intérieure des pays amis.

Nous rappellerons seulement qu’en Suisse, il n’existe pas de destitution d’un membre du gouvernement : le Parlement décide simplement, tous les quatre ans, de le réélire ou non. Et le peuple vote quatre fois par an, ce qui laisse très peu de temps pour les lettres de menace.

Lost in translation
  • Swiss federal votes are held four times a year; members of the Federal Council are elected by Parliament for four years and cannot be removed during their term.
