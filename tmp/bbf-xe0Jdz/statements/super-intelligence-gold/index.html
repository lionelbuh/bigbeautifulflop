Super Intelligence is GOLD

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 6 Nov 2026 · 7:01 AM ET

Original

As President, I am 100% in favor of Super Intelligence (SI), and will work with the many States in the U.S. that want to become RICH by having them. They are GOLD, and only foolish leadership will say “NO” to the riches they bring! President DJT

Source: Truth Social, @realDonaldTrump (via Roll Call Factbase)

Satire

My administration strongly supports the development of advanced artificial intelligence, and we will work with every state that wants to host this industry and the investment and jobs it brings.

Each state will make its own choice. I believe those that say yes will prosper, and I will make the case to the others with arguments, not insults.

Version française

Mon administration soutient résolument le développement de l’intelligence artificielle avancée, et nous travaillerons avec chaque État qui souhaite accueillir cette industrie, ses investissements et ses emplois.

Chaque État fera son choix. Je suis convaincu que ceux qui diront oui prospéreront, et je défendrai cette conviction auprès des autres par des arguments, pas par des insultes.

Satire

Alors moi, la super-intelligence, je suis pour. À cent pour cent. Bon, nonante pour cent. Faut voir.

Les cantons qui en veulent deviendront riches, paraît-il. C’est de l’or, qu’ils disent. Chez nous, l’or, on le met à la banque et on n’en parle pas.

Translate post

Right, super intelligence: I’m for it. A hundred percent. Well, ninety. We’ll see.

The states that want it will get rich, apparently. It’s gold, they say. Round here, gold goes in the bank and nobody talks about it.

And the ones who say no, I wouldn’t call them stupid. I’d say they have their reasons, wouldn’t you?
