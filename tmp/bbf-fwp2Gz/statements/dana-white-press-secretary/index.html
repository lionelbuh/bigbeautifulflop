A Press Secretary like none other

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 7 Nov 2026 · 2:30 PM ET

Original

I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States. I have requested that he bring with him assistants, even though some are not from our Country, of Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes, and Kayla Harris. We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left. Dana will be a Press Secretary like none other. Dana, please take the job! President DONALD J. TRUMP

Satire

I have offered the position of White House Press Secretary to Dana White, who built one of the most successful sports organizations in the world. I admire his energy and his directness.

The Press Secretary’s job is to inform the American people and to answer the press, including its hardest questions. I am confident Dana would bring candor to that podium, and I hope he will accept.

Version française

J’ai proposé le poste de porte-parole de la Maison-Blanche à Dana White, qui a bâti l’une des organisations sportives les plus prospères au monde. J’admire son énergie et sa franchise.

Le rôle du porte-parole est d’informer les Américains et de répondre à la presse, y compris à ses questions les plus difficiles. Je suis convaincu que Dana apporterait de la franchise à cette tribune, et j’espère qu’il acceptera.

English version

Nobody gets hurt and everyone goes home happy. If press conferences in Washington could go like that, we’d gladly send over one of our queens to help.
