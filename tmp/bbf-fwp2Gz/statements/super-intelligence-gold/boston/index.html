The Boston version

Boston (United States)Boston, Massachusetts

Satire

Super intelligence. Fine. We’ve had it here since 1636. It’s called Harvard. Then MIT showed up across the river to argue with it.

So yeah, we’re in. But you want to call somebody foolish? Get in line, pal. Around here we were doing that before the country existed.

In plain English

Boston is in favour, and reminds everyone that it has been home to world-class universities for centuries.

It is not impressed by insults either: Bostonians consider bluntness a local speciality, and an old one.
