The Texas version

Texas (United States)Austin, Dallas, Houston

Satire

Now that’s a press secretary. Dana White at the podium, a few world champions standing behind him with their arms crossed? In Texas we’d call that a town hall meeting.

Only question I’ve got: when a reporter says “follow-up question”, is that the end of the round, or the start of it?

It just wonders whether reporters’ follow-up questions will now come with a bell.
