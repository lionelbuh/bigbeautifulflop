Tonight I will sit down with 60 Minutes. I welcome the chance to answer hard questions and to speak directly to the American people about the work of the past two years: what we have achieved, and what remains to be done.

With the support of the Senate, there is still a great deal we can accomplish together, and I intend to make that case.

Avec le soutien du Sénat, nous pouvons encore accomplir beaucoup ensemble, et j’entends le démontrer.

Round here, we don’t judge a watch by what the watchmaker says about it on television: we wear it for a few years and see whether it runs slow. Sixty minutes, by the way, is exactly how long it takes to check.
