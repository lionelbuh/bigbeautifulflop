A Press Secretary like none other

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 7 Nov 2026 · 2:30 PM ET

Original

I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States. I have requested that he bring with him assistants, even though some are not from our Country, of Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes, and Kayla Harris. We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left. Dana will be a Press Secretary like none other. Dana, please take the job! President DONALD J. TRUMP

Source: Truth Social, @realDonaldTrump

The Liverpool version

Liverpool (United Kingdom)Liverpool, Merseyside

Satire

A press secretary who’s a fighter, with mates from all over the world to back him up? Lad, that’s not a press office, that’s a Saturday night in town.

Fair play to him, though. If he asks nicely, he can bring Khabib round ours for a brew.

In plain English

Liverpool finds the idea of a press office full of fighters very familiar: it sounds like a big night out.

Still, it’s a good-natured city: the fighters are welcome for a cup of tea.

In British English, a “brew” is a cup of tea, which suits Khabib Nurmagomedov, who does not drink alcohol.

Super Intelligence is GOLD

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 6 Nov 2026 · 7:01 AM ET

Original

As President, I am 100% in favor of Super Intelligence (SI), and will work with the many States in the U.S. that want to become RICH by having them. They are GOLD, and only foolish leadership will say “NO” to the riches they bring! President DJT

Source: Truth Social, @realDonaldTrump

The Liverpool version

Liverpool (United Kingdom)Liverpool, Merseyside

Satire

Super intelligence? Go ’ed then, we’ll have a bit of that. Gold, he says.

Listen, lad, we’ve heard “this’ll make yous all rich” before. The only thing that ever really did was four fellas with guitars. If it can write a song half as good, it’s welcome round ours.

In plain English

Go ahead, Liverpool will give AI a try.

But it is wary of promises of riches: in its experience, the only thing that truly put the city on the map was the Beatles. If AI can write a decent song, it’s welcome.
