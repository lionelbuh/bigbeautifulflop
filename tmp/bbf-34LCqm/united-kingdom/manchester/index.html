The Manchester version

Manchester (United Kingdom)Manchester

Satire

I’m on 60 Minutes tonight. Hopefully they show all the good bits of the last two years, and the first term, which was class, to be fair.

Mind, I know how telly works: you give them an hour and they keep the twelve minutes where you said something daft. So if the Senate could back me up, cheers, that’d be top.

In plain English

He hopes the interview shows his achievements, and calls his first term “class” (excellent, in Manchester English).

But he knows television editing keeps the embarrassing moments, so he asks the Senate to back him up.

The Manchester version

Manchester (United Kingdom)Manchester

Satire

Super intelligence? Yeah, we’re in. Bit late, mind. We built the first stored-program computer here in 1948, so technically it’s ours and he’s just catching up.

Calling folk foolish for saying no, though? Not very clever for a super intelligence fan, is it?

Manchester supports AI and points out that the world’s first stored-program computer was built there in 1948.

It also notes that insulting people is not a very intelligent way to promote intelligence.
