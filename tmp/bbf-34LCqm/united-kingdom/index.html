Satire

I’m on 60 Minutes tonight. Hopefully they show all the good bits of the last two years, and the first term, which was class, to be fair.

Super Intelligence is GOLD

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 6 Nov 2026 · 7:01 AM ET

Original

As President, I am 100% in favor of Super Intelligence (SI), and will work with the many States in the U.S. that want to become RICH by having them. They are GOLD, and only foolish leadership will say “NO” to the riches they bring! President DJT

Source: Truth Social, @realDonaldTrump

United Kingdom

Yorkshire (United Kingdom)Leeds, Sheffield, York

Satire

Gold, is it? We’ll see. In Yorkshire we don’t say no to riches, but we don’t say yes till we’ve seen the price. If it’s free, we’ll have two.

And as for calling folk foolish: round here, that word is reserved for a man who pays full price.

In plain English

Yorkshire will consider AI once it knows what it costs, and will happily take it if it’s free.

The only people Yorkshire calls foolish are those who don’t look for a bargain.
