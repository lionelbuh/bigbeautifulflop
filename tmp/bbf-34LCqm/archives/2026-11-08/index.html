Mind you, round here we don’t judge a watch by what the watchmaker says about it on TV: we wear it for a few years and see whether it runs slow. Me, I never run slow. Well, hardly ever. And if the Senate gives me a hand, we’ll do things that make every hand on the dial spin.

We would observe, gently, that one rarely chooses which parts of an interview are broadcast. That, after all, is rather the point of a free press.
