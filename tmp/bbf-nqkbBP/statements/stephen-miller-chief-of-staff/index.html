A new Chief of Staff

DT

Donald J. Trump@realDonaldTrump · Truth Social · 6 Nov 2026 · 1:51 PM ET

Original

I am pleased to announce that Mr. Stephen Miller, the current Deputy Chief of Staff of the Trump Administration, and one of my most trusted advisors, will become the Chief of Staff starting Monday, November 9, 2026. Susie Wiles, who has done an outstanding job, will be moving over to the Trump Political Operation. I want to thank her for her service as Chief of Staff — She has done a great job!
