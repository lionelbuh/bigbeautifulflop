About

An independent satirical publication

Lost in translation. Found in reality.

Big Beautiful Flop takes public statements by Donald Trump and shows them three ways: the original, word for word; the same message as a measured, statesmanlike president would put it; and the way people around the world would actually say it.

Our rules

  • The original is quoted verbatim, dated and sourced. No source, no statement.
  • We never invent quotes. Our versions are never attributed to Donald Trump and are always labelled as satire.
  • Same message, different manners. The presidential version keeps the facts and drops the insults.
  • Local means local. Each regional version is read by someone from the region before it is approved.
  • Humans publish. AI helps us draft; an editor approves everything.

Regions

  • Switzerland: Vaudois, Valaisan, Genevois, Fribourgeois, Neuchâtelois
  • France: Parisien, Sud de la France, Ch’ti, Lyonnais, Breton

Disclaimer

An independent satirical project. Original statements are sourced and attributed. All presidential and regional adaptations are fictional, AI-assisted editorial interpretations. Big Beautiful Flop is not affiliated with any government, party, campaign, news agency or social network.
