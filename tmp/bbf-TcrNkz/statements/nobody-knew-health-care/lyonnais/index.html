Nobody knew health care could be so complicated

DT

Donald J. TrumpWhite House remarks · 27 Feb 2017

Original

Now, I have to tell you, it’s an unbelievably complex subject. Nobody knew that health care could be so complicated.

Source: Remarks to the National Governors Association, 27 February 2017 · wording to be checked

The Lyonnais version

LyonnaisLyon

Satire

Personne savait que la santé, c’était si compliqué ? Oh, les gones, faut arrêter le beaujolais nouveau.

N’importe quel Lyonnais vous le dira : c’est plus simple de traverser la Presqu’île par les traboules sans se perdre que de comprendre une feuille de remboursement.

Bon, on en reparle au bouchon, autour d’un tablier de sapeur et d’un pot de côtes-du-rhône. Et après, promis, on réforme.

Translate post

Nobody knew health care was so complicated? Come on, guys, lay off the new wine.

Any Lyonnais will tell you: it’s easier to cross the old town through the secret passageways without getting lost than to understand a medical reimbursement form.

Right, let’s discuss it over lunch at the bistro, with some fried tripe and a jug of red. And after that, I promise, we reform.

draft
Lost in translation
  • “Gone” is Lyon slang for a kid, or a fellow Lyonnais of any age.
  • Traboules are the covered passageways that run through buildings in old Lyon. Tourists get lost in them; locals pretend not to.
  • A bouchon is a traditional Lyon bistro. The tablier de sapeur is breaded, fried tripe. A “pot” is a 46-centilitre bottle of house wine.
