We would have won in a landslide

Original

I wasn’t on the Ballot and if I was, we would have won IN A LANDSLIDE! Thank you for your attention to this matter. President DONALD J. TRUMP

Source: Truth Social, @realDonaldTrump

The Sud de la France version

Sud de la FranceMarseille, Provence

Satire

J’étais pas sur le bulletin. Mais si j’y avais été, peuchère, on gagnait cent mille à zéro. Que dis-je : un million.

Les autres, ils faisaient Fanny, devant tout le monde, sur la place du village. Et je vous le dis sans exagérer, moi je n’exagère jamais : on en aurait parlé jusqu’à Marseille, et même jusqu’à Toulon. Merci pour votre attention, et ça, c’est pas une galéjade.

English version

I wasn’t on the ballot. But if I had been, good heavens, we’d have won a hundred thousand to nil. What am I saying: a million.

The others would have had to kiss Fanny, in front of everybody, on the village square. And I tell you without exaggerating, I never exaggerate: they’d have talked about it all the way to Marseille, and even to Toulon. Thank you for your attention, and that’s no tall tale.

draft
Lost in translation
  • At pétanque, a player who loses 13–0 “fait Fanny”: by tradition they must kiss the bottom of Fanny, a picture or statue of a bare-bottomed woman kept in the café. “Peuchère” is a Provençal exclamation; a “galéjade” is a tall tale.
